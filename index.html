🧠 Worm Fun Facts:

🌍 There are over 1 million worm species on Earth!

❤️ Worms have 5 "hearts" (aortic arches) to pump blood.

👀 Worms don't have eyes, but they sense light!

🌱 A single worm can process 1/3 of its body weight daily.

🤝 Worms are hermaphrodites (both male and female)!
